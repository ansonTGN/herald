# Product Hunt Launch Strategy for Developer Tools

## Overview

Based on research from 3 "Top 5 Product of the Day" launches (GraphCMS, Specify, VideoSDK) and community insights from Indie Hackers, Reddit, and PH Discussions.

---

## Timeline

### 2-3 Months Before: Planning
- [ ] Define your launch angle (what makes Herald unique/timely)
- [ ] Identify and reach out to a Hunter (influential PH user to submit your product)
- [ ] Start building assets: screenshots, video, copy
- [ ] Begin building in public on X/Twitter

### 1 Month Before: Audience Building
- [ ] Team members become active on PH (upvote, comment on others' launches for 10+ days)
- [ ] Share development journey on Twitter/X, Reddit, Indie Hackers
- [ ] Build email list / pre-launch page
- [ ] Prepare all scheduled content (tweets, emails, DMs)
- [ ] Write and polish PH listing (tagline, description, maker comment)

### Launch Week: Final Prep
- [ ] Test all links and assets
- [ ] Prepare email templates for launch day
- [ ] Brief your support network (friends, team, community)
- [ ] Schedule social media posts in advance

### Launch Day
- [ ] **Go live at 12:01 AM PST** (Beijing time ~4:01 PM)
- [ ] First 30 min: get 100+ supporters to upvote
- [ ] If not featured after 5 min: contact PH support
- [ ] Respond to every comment with real conversation (not just "thanks!")
- [ ] Post launch thread on X/Twitter
- [ ] Share in communities (Reddit, Indie Hackers, Discord, Slack)

### Post-Launch
- [ ] Continue engaging with comments for 24-48 hours
- [ ] Share results on social media
- [ ] Follow up with interested users
- [ ] Collect feedback and plan next iteration

## Key Strategies for Developer Tools

### 1. Find a Hunter
- A well-known PH user submitting your product increases chances of being featured
- Reach out to Hunters in the developer tools space
- Not strictly required, but recommended for first-time launchers

### 2. Timing
- **Launch at 12:01 AM PST** (PH resets daily)
- **Consider weekends** — weekdays get dominated by big players (Google, OpenAI)
- Your goal: be in Top 3 within the first hour

### 3. The First 2 Hours Are Critical
- PH algorithm weights acceleration of upvotes throughout the day
- Have 100+ supporters ready to upvote within 30 minutes of launch
- Respond to all comments immediately — start conversations, not just thanks

### 4. Visual Assets Are Key
- PH is a surprisingly visual channel
- Show the product as much as possible
- Prepare gallery images, video, and GIF thumbnail

### 5. Pre-schedule All Content
- On launch day, you'll be focused on your PH dashboard
- You won't have time to create tweets, emails, or posts
- Prepare everything in advance and schedule it

### 6. Multi-Channel Promotion
- Product Hunt alone is not enough
- Reddit + Indie Hackers can drive 3-8x more signups than PH alone
- Post on: r/SaaS, r/buildinpublic, r/webdev, Indie Hackers, Hacker News
- Twitter/X thread with your story + product demo

### 7. Genuine Engagement
- Don't buy upvotes (PH detects and penalizes)
- Build real relationships, not just email lists
- Engage with comments by starting conversations
- Highlight the best comments on Twitter for compound effect

### 8. Build in Public
- Share your development journey on X/Twitter weeks before launch
- Having followers who are indie makers/developers amplifies reach significantly
- Tease the launch with a series of tweets to build momentum
