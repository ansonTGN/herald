# Product Hunt Copywriting Guide

## 1. Tagline (< 60 characters — PH truncates beyond this)

### 6 Proven Tagline Formulas

| # | Formula | Example (Real PH Launch) | Herald Candidates |
|---|---------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Show Promise** | "Use ChatGPT to become a top 1% copywriter" | "Add multi-tenant auth to your app in 5 minutes" |
| 2 | **Address Pain Point** | "Never be late again, built by a team with ADHD" | "Stop reinventing auth — we built it in Rust" |
| 3 | **Transformation** | "Turn boring product images into beautiful photos with AI" | "Turn auth headaches into a 5-minute setup" |
| 4 | **Use Numbers** | "Cut down meetings by 40%" | "Multi-tenant auth in 3 lines of code" |
| 5 | **Bold Statement** | "Ditch the grids, create websites like you design graphics" | "Auth that scales with your tenants" |
| 6 | **Brand Analogy** | "Gumroad + Linktree alternative for creators" | "Clerk meets Keycloak, built in Rust" |

### Tagline Rules
- Keep under 60 characters (never truncated)
- Strip heavy adjectives ("disruptive", "revolutionary", "game-changing")
- Test: show it to someone unfamiliar — can they guess what the product does?
- Prefer a full sentence over a fragment

## 2. Description

### Best Practices
- Keep scannable with bullet points and emojis
- Lead with the pain point or hook, then list features
- No stock images, no marketing fluff — show the product

### Proven Formats

**Format A: Pain Point + Feature List**
> We were sick of [pain point], so we built something better. [Product] does X, Y, Z. Available on [platforms].

**Format B: Tutorial + Ease of Use**
> Create [result] in 3 simple steps:
> 1. Design → [step 1]
> 2. Animate → [step 2]
> 3. Export → [step 3]
> All in your browser!

**Format C: Hook + Emoji Features**
> [Intro hook] 👇
> 🎥 [Feature 1]
> ✨ [Feature 2]
> 📺 [Feature 3]

## 3. Maker Comment (First Comment)

### Structure (Used by Top #1 Products)
1. **Background** — Why you built this product (personal story)
2. **Problem** — The industry pain point you're solving
3. **Solution** — How your product solves it
4. **Features** — 3-5 key highlights (use bold for key points)
5. **Offer** — Special deal for PH community (discount, credits, free tier)
6. **CTA** — Ask for feedback, provide links

### Herald Maker Comment Template
> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I'm [name], and I'm excited to share Herald with you today.
>
> **The problem:** Every time I started a new SaaS project, I'd spend weeks rebuilding the same auth system — user registration, MFA, role-based access, payment integration across different providers. It was always the same pain, and existing solutions were either too expensive, too opinionated, or couldn't handle multi-tenancy properly.
>
> **So we built Herald** — an open-source, multi-tenant authentication and authorization system built in Rust.
>
> Here's what it does:
>
> 🔐 **Full auth stack** — Registration, login, TOTP, password strength validation, Cloudflare Turnstile
>
> 🏗️ **Multi-tenant first** — True tenant isolation with RBAC permissions
>
> 💳 **Billing built-in** — Stripe, WeChat Pay, and Shopify integration out of the box
>
> 🦀 **Rust-powered** — High performance, memory safety, and a small footprint
>
> 🔌 **Easy integration** — Get started with just a few lines of code
>
> **PH Special:** [offer details]
>
> We'd love your feedback! What auth features are you missing in your current stack? 🙏
